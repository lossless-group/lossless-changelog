## Why Care?

The totals only counted repos with a changelog stream here. That left out a
lot of the writing: the context-v corpus, the content-areas and client-content
submodules, and the monorepo root itself. Three of the four are private, so
their entries can't be published, but nothing stops their work from counting.

## What's New?

- **A `stats_only:` list in `streams.yaml`.** Repos there count toward the
  headline, the strip, focus days, and the lines-by-kind bar, but get no
  stream, no entries, and no project row. Only totals leave them.
- **Four repos added**: `context-v-corpus` (context), `content-areas` and
  `lossless-client-content` (content), and `lossless-monorepo`.
- **They move on a push too.** Each is subscribed in [[lossless-hook-relay]],
  and so is `lossless-content`, which was counted but never woke the site.
- **The totals now read** 8,662 commits across 49 repos, with 455k lines of
  context, 148k of changelog, and 791k of content.

## Under the Hood

- **An `exclude:` list per repo** drops paths whose lines were written
  somewhere else. `context-v-corpus/corpus/` is a copy of every repo's
  context-v, 672k lines that would have been counted twice. The root's
  history still holds `site/`, `content/`, and `data/` from before they
  became their own repos.
- **Commits are excluded too, not just lines.** 182 of the root's 426 commits
  touched only those folders. A commit counts only once it touches a path
  outside the list, in the scan and in the live overlay alike.
- **Weekly series for those repos come from local history**, because GitHub's
  weekly stats can't leave paths out. `sync-line-classes.mjs` writes them
  into `_commits.json`, and `sync-commits.mjs` leaves them alone.
- **The hook knows stats-only repos.** A push to one forgets its activity and
  purges the cache even though it has no streams.
