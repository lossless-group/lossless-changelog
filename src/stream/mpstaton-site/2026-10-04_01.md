# Week 1 Closed, on the Cohort's Clock

## Why Care?

A 100-day plan is easy to write and hard to see. Week 1 of mine is now on the
dashboard in full, and the dashboard got better at answering the two questions my
coach and accountability buddy actually ask: *how far along are you,* and *what
happened there?*

## What's New?

- **Outcomes up top.** Each week view opens with a tile per outcome: latest
  reading, a meter from where I started to the goal, and where the current rate
  lands by Day 90. After-tax income reads about 77% of the way to $18k a month.
  Weight, VO2max, and BMI (my coach's focus) are waiting on readings.
- **Reasons you can open.** A miss can carry its why: click the ⓘ beside the
  status and a bubble opens right there. Week 1's outreach misses say what they
  were: no email system and no imported relationships yet.
- **Sunday-to-Saturday weeks.** The cohort's form is due Sunday afternoon, so the
  weeks now run on that clock. Week 1 was Mon–Sat; Week 2 started today.
- **Base and stretch,** the program's words, everywhere on the page.
- **Bonus is green** when done; habits with no bar this week show neutral.
- **Fasts count in hours.** The weekly fast is the longest continuous stretch,
  so a 24-hour fast reads as two-thirds of 36, not zero.
- **Check-ins count individually,** 0–2 a day.

## The Plan Itself

- Goals renamed: **BodyOS**, **PersonalOS**, Income, **Music & Songwriting**,
  **Relationships**.
- Goal 5 is now about family and close friends. My daughter lives in France, so
  presence with her moves to a Christmas trip, which lands around Day 89, right
  before the Day 90 checkpoint.
- New outcomes: VO2max and BMI; weight goal 190 lb by Day 90; Song Clock now in
  hours to a first cut (goal: one hour); LP indicated interest tracked.

## Under the Hood

The rollup logic and components come from the toolkit at
[hope-ai](https://github.com/lossless-group/hope-ai) (`references/rendering/dashboard/`),
where the same change shipped today; the site is where it gets proven on real data.
The fictional sample dataset was refreshed to show every cadence (daily, weekly,
biweekly, monthly) and a reading every week.
