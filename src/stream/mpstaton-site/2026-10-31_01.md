## Why Care?

The [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) asks very little of a folder of markdown: parseable frontmatter, a
non-empty `type` on every doc, and a table of contents in `index.md`. Meet
that, and any OKF tool can read the folder without knowing anything about how
we work.

Context-v is now an OKF profile, and the rule that makes it one is a single
field: every doc declares a `type`, which is the name of its folder. This repo
is part of a tree-wide sweep that brings older `context-v/` folders up to that
rule.

## What's New?

- `context-v/issues/Build-Time-Too-Long.md` ends its frontmatter with
  `type: Issues`, and
  `context-v/plans/Implement-the-Standard-Table-of-Contents.md` with
  `type: Plans`. Nothing else in them moved, and no body line changed.
- `context-v/index.md` declares `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists both folders.
- `issues/index.md` and `plans/index.md` list their doc, each with its lede
  as the one-line description.

Both docs already carry the kit's full baseline frontmatter, so this is the
only entry for the sweep here.

## Under the Hood

This is a compliance change, not a content change, so the docs keep their
dates. The edit was made by hand, one doc at a time; no script wrote
frontmatter.

The deck narratives under `context-v/narratives/` are gitignored and stay
local, so the sweep leaves them alone. The repo qualified because
`development`, `main`, and `master` were all at the same commit, so this lands
on all three at once.
