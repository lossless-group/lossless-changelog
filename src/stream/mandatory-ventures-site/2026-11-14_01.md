# Three Ways to Print the Same Page: Mandatory Ventures Gets a Site

## Why Care?

Mandatory Ventures is an old venture brand coming back into formation. It needed
somewhere to point people: one screen that makes large-scale capital want a
conversation about getting onto the cap tables of scaleups, through the
investors who earned that access early.

Rather than pick a look up front, we agreed the story first, found the look on a
mood board, and then built three complete, working directions against the same
words. Choosing between them is now a matter of clicking, not imagining. Retiring
the losers is a delete, not a refactor.

## What's New?

- **A signed-off narrative** (`context-v/narratives/`): hero, the redrawn curve,
  two audiences, why this one, a coalition rather than a company. No form, no
  call to action.
- **A mood board** (`design/moodboard/`) of 1960s corporate annual reports
  (Central Airlines, Northwest, Utah Construction, Bell Canada), Erik Nitsche's
  posters, Paul Rand, Gilliam-style collage, and interior spreads, not just
  covers. Every reaction is logged in `decisions.md`.
- **A brand, in tokens.** The north star is Goldsholl's parcel label for Paul
  Theobald and Company: lowercase italic serif, spaced geometric caps,
  typewriter, one red hairline rule. Content is typewriter black and pen blue;
  red is the editor's pen, for corrections and jokes.
- **Three directions**, each in light, dark and vibrant:

| Route | Direction | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| `/label/` | Label | Closest to the north star. The parcel label as the whole site. |
| `/report/` | Annual Report | A 1960s report: Nitsche banded cover, typed chart, ledger, silhouette plate. |
| `/paste-up/` | Paste-Up | The page mid-production: misregistered headline, halftones, crop marks, red pen. |

- **`/brand-kit`** and **`/design-system`** (switchable across all nine
  direction × mode combinations), plus a Mandatory Reading article template in
  each direction, rendered through `@lossless-group/lfm`.

## How it Works

The ordering is the trick (see the `generate-site-design-variants` skill):

```
narrative ──▶ mood board ──▶ decisions.md ──▶ Tier 1 tokens ──▶ 3 × Tier 2 themes
                                                   │
src/content/ (sections, voices, reading) ──────────┴──▶ /label  /report  /paste-up
```

All three directions read the same `src/content/` collections and `src/lib/site.ts`.
Each owns exactly one theme file, one component folder and one route, and never
imports from another. `scripts/check-word-budget.mjs` counts words in each
direction's built HTML against `WORD_BUDGET = 520`, so no direction can win by
inventing copy (Label 410, Annual Report 483, Paste-Up 450).

## Under the Hood

- **Print, not screen.** Flair is only allowed if it looks fought onto paper.
  Shared SVG filters (`PrintFilters.astro`) give lines a typewriter, felt-tip or
  ballpoint edge; `.grain`, `.overprint`, `.misregister` and `.faded-gradient`
  are shared utilities. No glossy gradients, glass or neon.
- **One gotcha:** overprint uses `screen` in dark mode so inks glow on dark
  stock, which wiped out dot screens printed on cream slips.
  `.overprint--on-paper` stays `multiply` in every mode.
- **Standalone install.** The site lives inside `astro-knots/sites/` but must
  deploy on its own: `.npmrc` with `ignore-workspace=true`, a site-local
  `pnpm-workspace.yaml` and `pnpm-lock.yaml`, and LFM pulled from JSR.
- Astro 7, Tailwind 4 via `@tailwindcss/vite`, no React, no JSX. `astro check`
  reports 0 errors, 0 warnings. Every page is `noindex` while the work is a spike.
