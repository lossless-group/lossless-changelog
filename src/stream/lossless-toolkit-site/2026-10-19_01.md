# The toolkit moves to toolkit.lossless.group

## Why Care?

This site is shared one link at a time, mostly in chat, so the unfurl card is
the first thing anyone sees. Every absolute URL the site printed, including
the share image, pointed at `lossless-toolkit.vercel.app`, a host that returns
404. The real deployment was at `lossless-toolkit-site.vercel.app`. So links
unfurled with no image, and the sitemap pointed search engines at nothing.
This was flagged on 2026-09-15 and is now fixed.

## What's New?

- **The custom domain is the canonical origin.** `toolkit.lossless.group` is
  attached in Vercel and serving over HTTPS. Canonicals, `og:url`, `og:image`,
  `twitter:image` and the sitemap now all use it.
- **One place defines the origin.** `astro.config.mjs` reads `SITE_URL` and
  falls back to the custom domain. `src/lib/seo.ts` used to keep its own copy
  of the origin and now reads Astro's `site`, so the two can't drift again.
- **robots.txt is generated.** It moved from `public/` to
  `src/pages/robots.txt.ts`, so its `Sitemap:` line follows `site` too. The
  crawler rules are unchanged, and the route is kept out of the sitemap.

## Under the hood

Checked against a production build: `dist/` contains no reference to the old
host, the home page's canonical and `og:image` name the custom domain, and
`/og/toolkit-card.jpg` returns 200 there. `pnpm test`: 18 passing.
