## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit has a baseline frontmatter template: `title`,
`date_created`, `date_modified`, `site_uuid`, `hex_code`, `authors`. Every
doc that carries all of them can be sorted, linked, and rolled up the same
way as every other doc in the tree.

This pass fills only what's missing, and only from facts. Nothing is
guessed, no existing value changes, and no date is set to the day of the
sweep.

## What's New?

- **`site_uuid` and `hex_code`** on all six docs: the three explorations,
  the handoff, the favicon issue, and the brand-rank reminder. Each pair was
  minted by `uuidgen` and `/dev/urandom` and checked against the whole tree
  for duplicates before it was pasted in.

Every doc already had its `title`, `date_created`, `date_modified`, and
`authors`, so those are unchanged. `semantic_version` stays as written; it's
a permanent alias, not a key to rename.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
