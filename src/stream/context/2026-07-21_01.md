## Why Care?

The context-vigilance skill's own philosophy says the convention grows by assimilation — unplanned folders appear, prove themselves, and get promoted. Today that mechanism ran for real, backed by a tree-wide survey: `plans/` exists in 14 context-v directories and `agent-skills/` in 5, all organic, none of it named by the skill. A convention document that trails its own practice by that much stops being the source of truth. This release closes the gap and — for the first time — gives the skill an explicit *experimental tier*, so proposed folders have a documented on-ramp instead of living in ambiguity until someone notices them.

## What's New?

- **Six → Eight canonical directories.** `plans/` joins Prep mode (spec → plan → prompt, in descending altitude — the natural home for plan-mode output, roadmaps, and migration sequences). `agent-skills/` joins Reflective mode as its executable tier: per-repo skills in the Anthropic SKILL.md shape, incubating next to the code they serve before graduating to this shared public repo.
- **Universal utility folders.** `extra/` (scratch and out-of-band material — now **gitignored by default**, making the privacy boundary structural rather than tooling-dependent) and `sitemap/` (maps of a project's own pages/routes/slides/surfaces, proven across the deck client-sites, applicable anywhere).
- **Experimental tier.** `loops/` (durable definitions of recurring operational loops — scope, cadence, exit conditions — pairing with agent loop-runners), `handoffs/` (end-of-session state capture so the next session, human or agent, resumes without re-derivation), `decisions/` (isolated artifacts of decisions made — what, when, why, alternatives passed over; kin to ADRs but not limited to architecture), and `contracts/` (binding agreements in two flavors: constitution contracts — standing rules agents must always follow — and interface contracts — ironclad API-level documentation of how data must flow through the app or a microservices architecture). Explicitly marked as not-yet-consistent: divergence between repos is expected and should be surfaced, not normalized.
- **Decision tree extended** to all twelve destinations, and the "seventh folder" section renamed to "a folder outside the set" — with the observed domain-specific folders (`narratives/`, `profiles/`, `inquiry/`, …) named as correct-where-they-are rather than drift.
- **References reconciled.** `philosophy.md` ("Six is not the answer" → "The count is not the answer", now citing plans/agent-skills as completed assimilations and loops/handoffs as the ones in flight) and `doc-type-guide.md` (tree updated to the eight).
