## Why Care?

An event is worth the ticket only if you walk in knowing who matters and why, and walk out with every touchpoint on a record. Until now that method lived in three sets of notes done informally (OceanX 2026, Cloudflare Connect 2026, Yotta 2026), so each new event started from scratch.

## What's New?

- **`event-prep/`**, marked draft. It covers the lifecycle from series note to prep map, the event itself, interaction logging and the recap re-sync.
- Four intake questions before any research: whose lens, what that lens wants from the room, attending or observing, and what the source list is.
- Routing by lens to the right folder (`content/sources/Events/` or `client-content/<client>/Events/`) and the right Twenty instance.
- Templates for the prep map in `references/templates.md`.
- CRM writes are proposed before they're made, and defer to `lossless-crm-interface-guidelines`.

## What's next

Refine it against the live RAISE Global Summit 2026 run, then drop the draft status.
