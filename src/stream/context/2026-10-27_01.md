## Why Care?

Every bare `[[link]]` clicked in Obsidian that resolves to nothing creates an empty note at the vault root, and real notes land there too when nobody files them. The root isn't under git, so a careless cleanup there can't be undone. This skill makes the cleanup safe and repeatable.

## What's New?

- **`obsidian-vault-hygiene/`**, marked draft.
- **Backup first:** every root note is copied out before anything moves.
- **Only confident moves:** candidates are sorted high, medium and low against the CRM and event rosters; only high moves, and medium goes to the operator.
- **Merge, don't duplicate:** an existing typed note absorbs the root note's aliases and any content.
- **Fill only what's empty:** OpenGraph fields and URLs are added from the firm's own site, never guessed, and existing values stay.
- **Links survive:** after moving, every basename is checked for uniqueness and ambiguous links are rewritten to full vault paths.

## First run

74 venture-firm notes promoted from the vault root into `vertical-toolkits/Venture-Capital-Firms/`: 68 moved, 6 merged into existing notes, all 126 inbound links still resolving.
