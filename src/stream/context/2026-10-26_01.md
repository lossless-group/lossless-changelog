## Why Care?

At a conference, the speaker you most need to understand is usually the one you're half-listening to while taking notes. The event app gives you a screenshot's worth of bio. This skill does the lookup in a fixed, phone-readable order, so the team walks up to someone knowing what they've built and said.

## What's New?

- **`augment-speakers-panelists/`**, marked draft.
- **Tool-agnostic research.** The skill never names a search tool. It asks event-hog (`get_config`) or a local `config/private.json` which tools this person and organization have, in order, and falls back to the chat app's own web search. If nobody has said, it asks once and saves the answer.
- **Identity first.** Name plus organization is the anchor; a same-name match without the organization is left out.
- **A fixed order:** social accounts, then features (about or by them), then mentions (quoted or named), then video.
- **Selection:** relevance to their session or panel first, then recency, capped at 10 features and mentions combined and 5 videos.
- **Only retrieved links are reported,** and what wasn't found is said out loud.
- **Saves to event-hog** (`save_speaker_profile`): a profile file in the event workspace, and one sourced observation per link in the agnostic-crm database.

## First run

Ari Chernikoff (Legacy Investing), speaking on converting a 1990 enterprise data center to 20MW of AI workloads. The run confirmed his identity, then found his LinkedIn, 4 features, 4 mentions, and a video. The finding that mattered: Legacy is building a 50MW AI-inference site 26 miles east of the conference's host city.
