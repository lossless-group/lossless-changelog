## Why Care?

The splash is where people read these skills, and its 61 skill and changelog pages
had no body text. The build still passed and every URL loaded, so nothing flagged it.

## What's New?

- **Bodies render through LFM,** installed from JSR, the same way as our other
  splashes.
- **Links work:** wikilinks go to the matching page here, and relative links to
  files like `references/` go to GitHub.
- **The skill now warns about this:** `maintain-splash-pages` says to render
  through LFM and to check a live page's text, not only its status.
