## Why Care?

Most events on the calendar are ones nobody will attend. Their rosters still say who's active, who's raising and who's backing whom. Awareness mode captures that without pretending anyone met anyone, and sets a date to check whether it paid off.

## What's New?

- **Awareness mode:** event records with no cost and no days spent, one *observed* participation per organization, never an interaction, minimal new company records flagged for review, and a grading date about 90 days out.
- **Research friction:**
  - Label every list by year; homepages often link last year's.
  - Parse server-rendered pages directly instead of converting them to markdown.
  - Get names from logo-only sections through image filenames or social posts.
  - Check series notes for co-hosted sibling events.
  - Match people on first *and* last name, and never substring-match short firm names.
- **Bulk-write pitfalls:** batch limits, deduping by domain and name, mapping roster firm strings to company records explicitly, and Obsidian's empty-stub links.
- **Reusable wording:** event names are out of the rules and headings. Slush 2026 and RAISE 2026, where these lessons were learned, are credited here instead.
