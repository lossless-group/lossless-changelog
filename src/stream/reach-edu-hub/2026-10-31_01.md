## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit now treats every `context-v/` folder as an
[Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) v0.2 bundle. The rule that makes that true is small: every doc
declares a `type`, the name of the folder it lives in. With it, any OKF
tool can read reach-edu-hub's `context-v/` without knowing anything about
our conventions.

This is a compliance change, not a content change. No body line, date,
or version in any doc was touched.

## What's New?

- **`type` added as the last frontmatter line** of all 22 docs:
  `Explorations` (2), `Narratives` (17, including the nested
  `citations/`, `pre-rag-synthesis/`, and `strategies/*/` docs), `Plans`
  (2), and `Sitemap` (1).
- **`context-v/index.md`**: the bundle's root index, declaring
  `okf_version: "0.2"` and listing the four folders.
- **A folder `index.md`** for `explorations/`, `narratives/`, `plans/`,
  and `sitemap/`, one line per doc using its lede, or its title where it
  has none (`citations/definitions.md`).
- The folder `README.md` files, the citation `registry.csv`, and the
  strategy one-pagers are left as they are.

The repo also gained its `development` and `master` branches, created at
`main`'s commit, so all three tiers now exist and agree.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
