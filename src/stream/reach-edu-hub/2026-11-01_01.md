## Why Care?

`status` is the field a workflow dashboard over every context-v groups by,
so it has to come from one shared list. The 2026-10-31 sweep ran before
that list was expanded; this pass brings reach-edu-hub's docs onto it,
applies the newer rule that a repo's own name isn't a tag, and closes a gap
that sweep left: folder READMEs with frontmatter but no `type`.

## What's New?

- **Five statuses moved onto the vocabulary**, each read against its doc:
  - Deck Collections exploration: `Seedling` to `Draft`. A full body with
    open questions; its shell-graduation proposal hasn't landed.
  - Google Slides export exploration: `Seedling — recommendation pending
    sign-off` to `In-Review`, which is what the doc says of itself.
  - Pipeline Building & Automation outline: `Outline` to `Draft`, a
    300-line outline.
  - Design-system plan: `Planned` to `Ready`. Phased work plan and
    acceptance criteria are written; no DESIGN.md exists yet, so nobody
    has started it.
  - `/` index page brainstorm: `Brainstorm` to `Draft`.
- **`tags`**: `Reach-Edu-Hub` comes out of the Deck Collections
  exploration, whose body names the repo.
- **`type: Narratives`** on ten READMEs (the citation registry, the pre-RAG
  synthesis index, and the eight strategy READMEs), each now listed in
  `narratives/index.md`.

No body line, version, or existing date changed.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
