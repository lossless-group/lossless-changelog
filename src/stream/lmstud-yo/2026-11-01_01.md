## Why Care?

`context-v/` holds the notes agents read before touching this plugin. It is now an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2 bundle, so any OKF reader can list what's here without opening each file.

## What's New?

- **The Obsidian-API reminder declares `type: Reminders`**, the name of its folder.
- **Its status reads `Active`**, the shared vocabulary's word for a rule in force (it said `Authoritative`).
- **`context-v/index.md`** marks the bundle with `okf_version: "0.2"`, and `reminders/index.md` lists the reminder with its lede.
- **Baseline metadata:** a `date_modified` from the file's last commit, and the AI credit as a one-item list.
