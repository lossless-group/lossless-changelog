## Why Care?

The [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) asks very little of a folder of markdown: parseable frontmatter, a
non-empty `type` on every doc, and a table of contents in `index.md`. Meet
that, and any OKF tool can read the folder without knowing anything about how
we work.

Context-v is now an OKF profile, and the rule that makes it one is a single
field: every doc declares a `type`, which is the name of its folder. This repo
is part of a tree-wide sweep that brings older `context-v/` folders up to that
rule.

## What's New?

- Every doc in `context-v/` now ends its frontmatter with its `type`:
  `Issues` (4), `Narratives` (16, including the deck's README), `Sitemap` (2),
  `Specs` (2), and `Tasks` (4). Nothing else in them moved, and no body line
  changed.
- `context-v/index.md` declares `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists the five
  folders.
- Each folder has an `index.md` listing its docs, each with its lede as the
  one-line description, or its title alone when it has no lede. The
  `narratives/` and `sitemap/` indexes group their docs by subfolder.

## Under the Hood

This is a compliance change, not a content change, so the docs keep their
dates. `date_modified`, `date_authored_current_draft`, and the version fields
are exactly as they were. The edit was made by hand, one doc at a time; no
script wrote frontmatter.

The repo qualified because `development`, `main`, and `master` were all at the
same commit, so this lands on all three at once.
