# Three directions, re-filled with the founder's Gateway rebuild

## Why Care?

The founder rebuilt her own site in parallel and liked where the three
directions were going. Her rebuild moved the positioning — oral **and** gut,
"start upstream", three ways in instead of one saliva test — and added pages,
a full blog, and real products. This update carries all of that into the
three directions without touching a single style: content and components only.

## What shipped

- **New positioning everywhere.** Orientation spine, "Why start here", proof
  points and brand name all come from her rebuild (`src/lib/site.ts`).
- **Three ways to begin** (`shared/Paths.astro`) on every homepage — test
  kits, guided support, Upstream — with a bridge to the start guide.
- **Twelve secondary pages** from one route, `pages/[direction]/[page].astro`:
  her starting-point guide (`shared/StartGuide.svelte`), About with all
  seventeen people (`shared/TeamGrid.astro`), the three series
  (`shared/SeriesCards.astro`) and the practitioner programme, each in its
  direction's chrome via `shared/DirectionShell.astro`.
- **Her whole blog.** All 61 articles imported from the data her site ships,
  at her own `/blog/<slug>` paths; the old `/articles` and her top-level page
  paths redirect.
- **Real test kits.** The three invented tiers are gone. The `bundles`
  collection now holds her four kits (Orobiome, KBMO Fit 22, Deep Sleep,
  Explore Complete) at real prices, linked to their product pages.

## The cart change worth keeping

Her kits are not a ladder — each is a different mix of tests and people — so
`BundleLadder` no longer assumes strict supersets. Every line across every kit
stays in the list; clicking a line moves you to the cheapest kit that has it;
and any switch states what it adds **and what it drops**. Where kits do nest,
nothing is ever listed as dropped and it behaves exactly as the old upgrade
ladder did. The superset-ladder blueprint is still right for catalogues that
nest; this is the general case.

## Open questions for the client

- Her guide's routing for mixed answers is not observable; ours is inferred
  (majority across Q1–Q3, ties to Q2).
- Her shop quotes the Explore tier at both $1,450 and $1,780; we used $1,780.
- One article byline/bio mismatch (`gut-health-and-weight-loss`).
- Her blog data has edit dates only, no publish dates.
