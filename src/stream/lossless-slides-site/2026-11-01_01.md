# Two Decks Made for Hypernova Capital

## Why Care?

Two ideas that do real work in how Hypernova Capital invests were sitting in plain Markdown slides. One of them, Networked Capital, was a single slide. Now both are decks you can actually present: one on why more value comes from exchange across organizations than from small teams working in isolation, and one on how to make judgement about early opportunities repeatable.

## What's New?

- **[Networked Capital, a Primer](https://lossless-decks.vercel.app/presentations/networked-capital).** Nine slides on Open Innovation in finance, power laws, the conundrums of the fund of funds, and a Fund-with-Funds angle on venture.
- **[Unique Judgement](https://lossless-decks.vercel.app/presentations/unique-judgement).** Seventeen slides covering the 12Ps within five questions, Mission Catalysts, a 12-dimension framework for evaluating GPs, and the scoring scale.
- **Both play in light, dark, and vibrant,** and link to each other at the close.

## The Story

The two sources were lopsided. Unique Judgement already had the words, so its remake was mostly a layout problem. Networked Capital had almost none, so we gathered its argument from copy that already existed across the Hypernova site rather than writing new claims.

Unique Judgement also listed five questions and only answered four. Offering never got a slide, because in the 12Ps it has no Ps of its own: it asks how coherently Proposition, Positioning, and Product line up. It has a slide now.

We wrote each outline before any layout, then generated each deck whole, per [[Generate-Whole-Decks-and-Regenerate-Fresh]]. Before shipping, we rendered every slide in all three modes at 1440×900 and 1280×720 and measured for overflow. Nothing spilled.

## Under the Hood

- Each deck is one registry entry in `src/lib/decks.ts`, one page, and one `v1/` folder of slide components. The gallery picks them up from the registry.
- Unique Judgement's four standard O slides share a local `OSlide.astro`, so the framework reads as one instrument. It stays inside the deck's folder, not in shared components.
- `astro check` did not run for this work: `@astrojs/check` isn't installed in this repo. `pnpm build` passes.
