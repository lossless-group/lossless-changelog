## Why Care?

The [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) asks very little of a folder of markdown: parseable frontmatter, a
non-empty `type` on every doc, and a table of contents in `index.md`. Meet
that, and any OKF tool can read the folder without knowing anything about how
we work.

Context-v is now an OKF profile, and the rule that makes it one is a single
field: every doc declares a `type`, which is the name of its folder. This repo
is part of a tree-wide sweep that brings older `context-v/` folders up to that
rule.

## What's New?

- The three blueprints in `context-v/blueprints/` now end their frontmatter
  with `type: Blueprints`. Nothing else in them moved, and no body line
  changed.
- `context-v/index.md` declares `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists the one
  folder.
- `context-v/blueprints/index.md` lists all three, each with its lede as the
  one-line description.

All three already carry the kit's full baseline frontmatter, so this is the
only entry for the sweep here.

## Under the Hood

This is a compliance change, not a content change, so the docs keep their
dates and versions. The edit was made by hand, one doc at a time; no script
wrote frontmatter.

This repo had only `main` on GitHub. The sweep first created `development`
and `master` at the same commit, so all three tiers exist and this lands on
all three at once.
